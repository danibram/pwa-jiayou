# Vocabulary interface review

The app ships the full HSK 2.0 exclusive word lists for levels 1–6. Character learning and its existing level packs remain available; word audio and strokes reuse those packs.

## Review rounds

1. The first mobile pass found a detached home action, an unimplemented topic entry, weak progress feedback, and no explicit recovery path for a session from a newer version. The home action now sits inside the daily panel. The topic entry opens a topic list. Home reports seen and mastered words and today's work. An unreadable saved session needs confirmation before deletion.
2. The character card comparison showed that the word on the review sheet was too small. The word now uses the existing rice-grid visual treatment, with one grid per character in the same word. The layout was reduced for 320 × 568 after the first version pushed controls below the card. Word detail shows its progress state and offers a contextual lesson or practice action. Repeated characters show their positions.
3. A saved daily queue could still teach a word learned in an individual session. The queue now drops stale introductions, and an already graded recall does not move its schedule again. The independent reviewer repeated the sequence and confirmed the word was skipped.
4. An independent reviewer checked the 320 × 568 word detail, practice prompt and revealed answer. The reviewer found no P1 or P2 layout issues in those states, then rechecked the three-character word 出租车 at 320 × 568 light and 390 × 844 dark. The reviewer requested free practice across the pilot. A separate deck now covers all 40 words without changing review dates or replacing the daily session. The reviewer verified retry and resume on mobile.

5. Word page and character drawer redesign, judged against `docs/vision/palabra-y-caracter.md`. The page now shows one headline sense, the example sentence with the word underlined, curated relations drawn by kind (synonym tiles, antonym pairs, the classifier phrase 一本书), sound-alike words and one card per character with its family. The drawer gained a radical card with other HSK characters that carry it, and its sound-alike section now includes same-tone homophones. An independent reviewer read 390 × 844 light, 320 × 568 dark and the drawer, and reported 12 findings (3 High, 6 Medium, 3 Low). Fixed: antonym pinyin and capitalisation, the stray related marker, chips moved inside each character card, the bare one-character word dropped from its own family, a dark-mode hole behind the character glyph, radical chips capped at six and lightened, a boxed word list in the drawer, an item shown twice when it was both an antonym and easy to confuse, copy ("pueden confundirse", "Fácil de confundir", "Ver radical y trazos") and the rules document gaps. Kept on purpose: the whole-word traditional form on the page (the rule was updated). Deferred: tone 3 and the radical are both green across the app; the sticky header has no edge when content scrolls under it. Relations were seeded on five words for the screenshots and removed afterwards.

6. A fresh reviewer looked only at the word page, with full-page screenshots of 时间 and 保证 (light, 390 wide) and 大 and 书 (dark, 320 wide), using seeded relations, sentences and the new HSK 4 to 6 meanings. 12 findings (2 High, 6 Medium, 4 Low). The High ones: a word could appear twice on the page (图书馆 as a related word and again in the family of 书), and the sentence characters could not be tapped. Fixes: the family now leaves out every word already shown above it. Every hanzi in the sentence opens its drawer. A shared `WordRef` tile replaced three different ways of drawing a word, and a one-item group takes the full row. A one-character word no longer repeats its hero in a second card; the section becomes "Palabras con X". The character rows lost the "Ver radical y trazos" text that cut the gloss, the family tiles gained pinyin, the traditional form moved to a quiet line, "Nueva" disappeared from the card because the Aprender button already says it. 7. A second fresh reviewer checked the result and found one real bug. The word in the sentence was not marked, because each character is now a button (inline-block) and the underline on `<mark>` did not reach it. The underline is now a border on each button, and the rest of the sentence is in faint ink; a test checks it. The family words now hang from a line under their character, so 时 and 间 read as two groups instead of one list of eight tiles. Suena parecido went back to small centred tiles in one shape at any count, so it no longer looks like the family. The antonym pair drops the gloss the hero already shows. Per-character glosses use one capitalisation. The owner then removed the tap hint altogether, because long meanings need that space in the card. Without it, a one-character word had no visible way into the drawer, so its section is "El carácter" again with one row that shows what the hero does not: the radical in jade, its meaning and the stroke count. Relations, sentences and the long HSK 4 to 6 meanings were seeded for both rounds and removed afterwards.

8. Applying the new HSK 4 to 6 meanings broke five quality rules, because those rules were written for a `meaning` that was the card's text. A first attempt split the field into a short `meaning` and a full `definition`. The owner rejected it: many words have several uses and categories, and none of them is the short one. `meaning` now holds every use. The word card shows only the word (glyphs, pinyin, traditional form) and its state as a chop in the corner: 新 new, 复 due today, 学 learning, 熟 mastered, in the app's status colours, with a tap that says what it means. Every use is listed right under the card, grouped by part of speech, the first one larger and bracketed notes in faint ink. Review cards, lists and small tiles show the first use without its notes (`cardSense` in `apps/web/src/utils/senses.ts`), and the quality rules now check that text. 37 first uses were reworded to fit a card: long classifier explanations moved their examples into brackets, leading articles were dropped, and 趁 had an unclosed bracket. The topic coverage rule was split: themes and tags still need three HSK 1–2 words, while a subtopic needs eight words of any level, because Sabores, Cocina, Muebles and Turismo have no honest HSK 1–2 candidates.

9. The owner asked for structured data instead of rules that read a string. Words now carry `senses`, a list of `{ pos, text, note }` with a closed list of twelve parts of speech, classifier included. A script split the 5005 meanings into 8643 uses, moved bracketed notes out of the text and kept the 1080 parts of speech the source already named, applying a tag to the untagged uses after it. Ten Haiku agents then labelled the other 7563 uses; they only chose a part of speech, never rewrote text, and the apply step rejected any value outside the list (none were). The word page groups uses by part of speech, the editor edits one use per row (part of speech, text, note, order), and review cards, lists and tiles read the first use's text. No view parses text any more. Known inconsistency to review in the editor: one batch tagged every chengyu as "expresión", while the others tagged chengyu by how the Spanish works (verbo, adjetivo).

## Learning session flow

The owner found the session abrupt: a right answer flashed for 800 ms and moved on, the answer card showed only the first meaning, the characters left the session for Trazos, and the summary was an overlay. The goal for these rounds: stop on a word the learner does not know with everything about it, let a known word go by on its own unless the learner holds it, look anything up without leaving, mobile first. Each round a fresh reviewer read 390 × 844 light and dark and 320 × 568 light screenshots of every state, captured headless with `agent-browser`, and did not see earlier rounds.

1. 15 findings (5 High). Known and missed answers looked the same; the countdown was an 11 px caption inside Continuar; reduced motion emptied the line at once while the 4 s timer ran; at 320 only two options were visible and the wrong-answer line fell below the fold; the summary read like a ledger. Fixes: the auto-continue moved from the store to the UI and runs only after a first-time right answer, Bien or Fácil, or a right typed answer, and it counts down in whole seconds so reduced motion works; the result now leads the card; at short heights the hero shrinks and meaning options sit in two columns; practice no longer prints "no cambia cuándo vuelve" on every card (the summary says it once, only when a session mixes reviews and practice); copy moved to the present perfect ("Has elegido «casa». Era «yo»."); sheets got a close button and a quiet "Ver trazos"; the summary leads with what the learner did, two facts for tomorrow, and one chip per word grouped into Para repasar, Bien and Vistas por primera vez. False positive: the title changing between "Práctica" and "Hoy" was two different sessions.
2. 12 findings (3 High). At 320 the details row fell off the card; the summary said "Has aprendido 15 palabras" over a list of misses (it counted lessons, a word taught twice counted twice); sheets had no way back. Fixes: the session summary now marks which items were taught (`taught` in `SessionItemResult`) and says "Has visto N palabras nuevas"; the audio button sits beside the pinyin; sheets stack with a back arrow; labels became "Otros usos" and "Aparece en"; a missed practice card says "Vuelve en unas tarjetas."; miss chips are neutral with a seal dot. Kept: "Quedan" rises after a miss on purpose; a "Repasar las N ahora" button needs a new session mode.
3. 10 findings (1 High). "No lo sé" and "Ya la sé" sat where Continuar had just been, so a fast second tap logged a miss. They now ignore taps for 400 ms on a new card. The countdown text broke the owner's no-instructions rule and changed the bottom height between cards; it is now a line on the top edge of Continuar. The summary fade was an overlay lighter than the textured paper; it is a mask now. The word sheet from the summary shows the meaning under the reading and the session result.

The owner then changed three things directly: no "toca el carácter" text anywhere (the characters section is always there); the back arrow, without text, moved into the progress row in place of the title bar; and the details fold became a scroll-down arrow on the card, with the whole word sheet under the card in the same scroll and Continuar fixed at the bottom.

4. A fresh reviewer read the new layout: 8 findings, none High. Its top one, the arrow not scrolling, came from the capture script scrolling the old element; the arrow now scrolls its own container and shows only while part of the sheet is out of sight. Fixes: summary rows show each word's meaning; the word sheet from the summary reuses the session's sheet (characters, "Aparece en"); undo got its own arrow icon instead of the replay circle; a held countdown tells screen readers the card waits; short screens get tighter card padding and every question is centred. Open: the character sheet promises a green radical even when stroke data is missing and the plain glyph shows instead.

## Verification

- `npm run check` passed 151 tests and built the production PWA.
- The production preview registered a service worker, reloaded with the browser offline, and opened a word lesson.
- Browser checks covered mobile and short landscape layouts, word search without pinyin spaces, lesson navigation and reload. In the offline preview, ten introductions led to recall, a missed answer added a retry, and reload restored the feedback. Tests cover teaching, recall, retry, session recovery, independent word practice and backups.
- Screenshots from the review are in `/tmp/jiayou-vocabulary-qa/`, including `characters-card.png`, `round5-small-light.png`, and `round3-review-desktop.png`. These are local review artifacts and are not part of the repository.

## Follow-up fixes

- Individual and free-practice sessions from a newer version now require confirmation before deletion. Failed deletion leaves the session in place.
- A word audio failure now shows a message when the fallback voice cannot play. The reviewer checked the failure with an English-only voice.
- Pinyin rendering no longer imports the character search index. Character search loads on demand. The production build split the previous 805 kB initial script into 435 kB initial and 374 kB for character search, and the chunk-size warning cleared.
- The reviewer checked free-practice retry and resume at 320 × 568. The practice did not mark an unseen word as learned.

## Limits

Audio for a syllable without a bundled recording depends on an installed Chinese voice or a downloaded character pack. The offline check covers the app, a word and its HSK 1 stroke data, not every audio combination or downloaded character pack. Topic tags still cover only a subset of level 1.
